# Disclaimer

## No Legal Conclusions

Nothing in any Body of Evidence investigation constitutes legal advice, a legal determination of guilt or liability, or a finding with legal force. Investigators are not lawyers. Findings are based on available evidence assessed against the platform's methodology — they are not equivalent to judicial determinations.

## No Medical or Scientific Conclusions Beyond Evidence

Where investigations touch on medical, scientific, or technical subjects, findings reflect what the available documented evidence supports. They do not constitute medical advice, scientific consensus, or expert opinion unless those elements are themselves documented in primary sources included in the investigation.

## Accuracy

We work to ensure that evidence is accurately extracted from sources, that sources are correctly attributed, and that assessments are honestly made. Despite these efforts, errors may occur. If you identify an error, please file a **Bug Report** issue. Documented errors are corrected through the revision process.

We do not guarantee that all relevant evidence on any subject has been found. Investigations are bounded by their stated scope and the sources available to contributors.

## Living Document

Investigations evolve as new evidence becomes available. A finding that is correct today may require revision tomorrow. Confidence levels reflect the state of the evidence at the time of assessment. The versioned history of every investigation is preserved.

## No Targeting

This platform does not exist to harm any individual. Where individuals are named, it is because they are relevant to the documented evidence. The inclusion of a person in an investigation is a factual record of relevance, not an accusation.
