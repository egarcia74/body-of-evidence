# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Body of Evidence is an evidence platform. Its value depends on contributors engaging in good faith, with intellectual honesty, and with respect for the people who are subjects of investigations and for each other.

We are committed to making this platform welcoming to contributors regardless of age, background, nationality, political affiliation, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, or level of experience.

## Standards

**Expected behaviour:**

- Engage with evidence, not with people's identities
- Critique methodology, not contributors
- Disclose conflicts of interest before contributing to related investigations
- Accept that rigorous methodological standards apply to everyone equally
- Disagree through the documented processes (challenges, revisions, discussions)
- Acknowledge and correct your errors

**Unacceptable behaviour:**

- Personal attacks, harassment, or intimidation
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information about contributors or subjects
- Submitting knowingly false evidence
- Attempting to manipulate the evidence model to support a predetermined conclusion
- Targeted harassment of a subject of an investigation outside the platform's documented processes
- Discrimination on any basis unrelated to the quality of a contribution

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer team (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for contacts). All reports will be investigated. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter.

**Sanctions (in order of severity):**
1. Written warning
2. Temporary suspension from contribution
3. Permanent ban

Serious violations (doxxing, fabricating evidence, sustained harassment) will result in immediate permanent ban without prior warning.

Maintainers who violate this Code of Conduct are subject to the same process, with enforcement by remaining maintainers.

## Appeals

Decisions may be appealed by contacting the maintainer team. Appeals are reviewed by a maintainer not involved in the original decision.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, the repository itself. It also applies when a contributor is representing the project in public.
